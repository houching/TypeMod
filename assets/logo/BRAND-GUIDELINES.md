# SegoeUI Changer — Brand & Logo Guidelines

**Concept:** **A3 — Soft-Corner Swap**  
Two opposing soft-cornered stepped glyph-bars frame a crisp 45° swap channel that forms an implied `S` — representing system font replacement, Latin + Khmer glyph merging, and typographic precision.

## 2. Colour Palette (Aligned with Vantage Graphite)

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Accent | Vantage Cobalt | `#4C77F7` | `76, 119, 247` |
| Light-Mode Ink | Vantage Graphite Ink | `#15181D` | `21, 24, 29` |
| Dark-Mode Ink | Vantage Off-White | `#EDEEF0` | `237, 238, 240` |
| One-Colour | Pure Black / Pure White | `#000000` / `#FFFFFF` | `0,0,0` / `255,255,255` |

- Use **2-colour** (`#4C77F7` + `#15181D` on light, `#4C77F7` + `#EDEEF0` on dark) as the primary mark.
- Use **1-colour** (`black`, `white`, or `mono-4c77f7`) when printing, embossing, or placing over busy imagery.

---

## 3. Clear Space & Minimum Size

- **Clear space:** Maintain at least **`0.25×` symbol width** (`64` units on the `256` grid — equivalent to the bar thickness) on all four sides of the symbol or lockup.
- **Minimum size:**
  - Symbol (standard): `24 px` digital / `8 mm` print.
  - Symbol (small-size cut / `.ico`): `16 px` digital.
  - Horizontal lockup: `120 px` wide minimum.

---

## 4. Misuse (Do Not)

- Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the mark.
- Do not change the 45° angle of the central swap channel or narrow it below the small-size cut.
- Do not apply drop shadows, outer glows, or unapproved gradients.
- Do not swap `#4C77F7` for unapproved colours or place the light-mode `#15181D` lower bar on a dark `#0D0F12` background.
